## Test follow-up

The final tests cover the gaps from my first review: first-dispatch re-entry, retry after a transport-owned initial failure, inherited default retry on a real stream-backed relay connection, and release of held promises even if executor termination fails. Netty cases use real TCP/Noise/Yamux connections with deterministic outbound close failures or held promises and exercise public APIs. The relay case uses three hosts and verifies Ping over the relayed stream before closing. It now also checks that ordinary relay close succeeds after retry and does not submit another stream close, while the shared connection to the relay remains open. Cleanup removes test handlers on the channel event loop and stops hosts in `finally` paths.

The parent reports that the old-helper baseline produced nine expected runtime failures and two expected boundary/default passes, and that the final targeted and neighboring runs passed. I did not rerun those commands. I found no additional concrete test isolation, race, or resource-cleanup defect in the current snapshot.
