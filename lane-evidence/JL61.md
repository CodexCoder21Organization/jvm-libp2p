OBSERVED: closeNettyChannelOnce publishes one private owner future in a channel attribute and returns derived caller futures. ConnectionOverNetty, PlainNettyTransport, and QuicTransport share it. A failed owner is never replaced. There are two Connection implementations: ConnectionOverNetty and RelayTransport.ConnectionOverStream; ConnectionStub is a fixture. No new call will be added to ordinary shutdown paths.

Mechanism to fix: the per-channel close owner retains its failed result forever, and Connection offers no explicit path to submit another physical close. Reproducer: real TCP/Noise/Yamux peers with a Netty outbound handler failing the first physical close, then permitting close; ordinary close retains its failure while the explicit operation must submit attempt two and close the socket.

Invariant table (before implementation):
