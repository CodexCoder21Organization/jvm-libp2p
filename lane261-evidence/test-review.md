Reviewed the production diff and all three new test classes against the brief's invariants. Tests drive real MultistreamProtocolV1 setup through public ProtocolBinding / ProtocolHandler / ProtocolMessageHandler APIs and real Netty embedded or TCP channels. No reflection or mock was added. The real TCP test starts isolated loopback hosts, forces a known record boundary independent of OS TCP segmentation, checks exact selection announcement counts, and proves the opened stream transports application data for both Mplex and Yamux.

Review additions: one new parameterized outgoing-write test (two rows); expanded an existing after-activation close test from two pending-controller rows to four rows by adding already-ready controllers for both roles. The original pending rows retain their exact expected done/exceptional/callback behavior. No existing callback or refCnt assertion changed. The new ready-controller rows require successful future completion to remain successful on close. Explicit repeated close strengthens idempotence coverage. Exact before/after diff is saved in final-review.diff.

No persisted state or restart contract exists on this path. Ordering, close, and callback reentry are forced directly; scheduler-load amplification, machine-specific tuning, new sleep, retry, and changed iteration/timeout limits are unnecessary and absent. Buffers are either consumed/released by real inbound handlers or explicitly released from the test's outbound queue; each case closes its channel or both TCP hosts in finally. Final verification uses ten independent 128 MiB test JVMs and paranoid Netty leak detection, plus explicit ownership assertions.
